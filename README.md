<div align="center">
  <h2>Recipes</h2>
  
  <h4>Enderite Scrap</h4>
  <p>You can smelt Raw Enderite in a furnace or a blast furnace to get Enderite Scrap.</p>

  <img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/bwbjkCOh/images/f6ecf4a5677554d3e1c5ce617b215f6beb73f13b.png" />

  <img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/bwbjkCOh/images/4d3e7e0db101f8c02b7d2c504b680042a927c459.png" />

  <h4>Enderite Ingot Recipe</h4>
  <p>You can get <strong>Enderite Ingot</strong> by combining 4 <strong>Enderite Scrap</strong>, 1 <strong>End Stone Block</strong>, 2 <strong>Chorus Flower</strong> and 2 <strong>Popped Chorus Fruit</strong> in the <strong>Crafting Table</strong>.</p>

  <img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/bwbjkCOh/images/7e6d1ba631a4acfd8f32ebe9714e8516cfdca7f8.png" />
